# 🎯 What Is a Web Service? - L1

## Question
>
> **Core Question:** Can you describe what a web service is?
> **Follow-up:** Give examples of services you have worked on.
> **Category:** Web Services

## 💡 Quick Answer (30 seconds)

A web service exposes business capabilities over a network via standard
protocols (usually HTTP), allowing different systems to communicate.

## 📖 Detailed Explanation

- **Contract-based communication:** Clients call endpoints with structured data.
- **Interoperability:** Different platforms/languages can integrate.
- **Common styles:** SOAP, REST, and RPC-like APIs.
- **Typical examples:** Authentication service, product/catalog service,
  notification service, payment integration facade.
