# 🎯 Partial Views in MVC - L2

## Question
>
> **Core Question:** Explain Partial Views in ASP.NET MVC and provide examples of their usage.
> **Category:** MVC

## 💡 Quick Answer (30 seconds)

Partial Views are reusable view components that render a portion of HTML markup. They enable modular view design, code reusability, and separation of concerns by breaking large views into smaller, manageable pieces.
